How to use

  • Add games with league, week, teams, and spread. Each game starts with your default resources.
  • Edit any resource row: choose winner, enter win probabilities, margin (+ for home, - for away), and spread pick.
  • Reorder or remove resources per game, or add new ones with the "Add Resource" button.
  • Summaries update instantly: pick counts, average margin, and spread cover tallies highlight who has the edge.
  • Use filters at the top to focus on a league or specific week/label.
